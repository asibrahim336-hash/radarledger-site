1. Controller

RadarLedger is operated by [REGISTERED NAME — sole trader], a sole trader registered in Dublin, Ireland. For data protection enquiries, contact: ahmed@radarledger.com.

2. What We Do

RadarLedger provides competitive intelligence services to business-to-business (B2B) clients in the direct-to-consumer e-commerce sector. We monitor publicly available competitor pricing, promotions, and advertising activity and deliver scored alerts and daily snapshots to our clients.

3. Data We Process

In the course of identifying potential B2B clients, we collect and process the following categories of business contact data:

  • Business name and trading domain
  • Business contact email addresses (typically role-based or publicly listed on company websites)
  • Business telephone numbers (where publicly listed)
  • Job title and professional role of decision-makers (where publicly available)
  • Country of establishment
  • Company size (staff range, from public sources)

We do not process personal data of consumers. All data subjects are business professionals whose contact details are sourced from publicly accessible web pages, company websites, business directories, and professional networks.

4. Legal Basis

We rely on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) as our legal basis for processing business contact data for the purpose of B2B outreach.

Balancing note: We have conducted a legitimate interest assessment. Our interest in contacting business professionals about a service directly relevant to their commercial operations is balanced against the minimal privacy impact of processing business contact data that is already publicly available. We limit processing to business-context data only, we do not contact individuals using personal (non-business) contact details, and we honour opt-out requests same day.

5. Sources

Business contact data is sourced exclusively from publicly accessible sources, including:

  • Company websites (contact pages, team pages, footer information)
  • Business directories and industry listings
  • Professional networking platforms (publicly visible profiles)
  • Public company registries

6. Retention

We retain business contact data for the duration of an active client relationship or active prospecting period. Where a prospect does not engage, their data is reviewed and deleted within 12 months of last contact attempt. Suppression records (opt-out requests) are retained indefinitely to ensure continued compliance with the opt-out.

7. Opt-Out and Suppression

You may opt out of receiving communications from RadarLedger at any time by replying to any message with a removal request, or by emailing ahmed@radarledger.com. Opt-out requests are honoured same day: your details are added to our permanent suppression list, which is checked before every outbound communication is sent. Once suppressed, you will not be contacted again by any channel.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email ahmed@radarledger.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. For Ireland, this is the Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

10. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or any form of visitor tracking. No data is collected from visitors to this website.
