2. What we do

RadarLedger provides competitive intelligence services for direct-to-consumer e-commerce brands. We monitor publicly accessible competitor storefronts and advertising activity, and deliver scored alerts and daily summaries to our clients.

3. Data we process

In the course of B2B outreach to prospective clients, we process:

  • Business name and website URL
  • Business contact email addresses (typically role-based or publicly listed)
  • Business telephone numbers (where publicly listed)
  • Job title and professional role (where publicly available)
  • Country of establishment
  • Company size (staff range, from public sources)

We do not process personal data of consumers. All data subjects are business professionals whose contact details are sourced from publicly accessible web pages.

4. Legal basis

We rely on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for processing business contact data for B2B outreach.

Balancing note: Our interest in contacting business professionals about a service directly relevant to their commercial operations is balanced against the minimal privacy impact of processing business contact data that is already publicly available. We limit processing to business-context data only, do not contact individuals using personal contact details, and honour opt-out requests same day.

5. Sources

Business contact data is sourced exclusively from publicly accessible sources:

  • Company websites (contact pages, team pages)
  • Business directories and industry listings
  • Professional networking platforms (publicly visible profiles)
  • Public company registries

6. Retention

We retain business contact data for the duration of an active client relationship or prospecting period. Where a prospect does not engage, their data is reviewed and deleted within 12 months of last contact attempt. Suppression records (opt-out requests) are retained indefinitely to ensure continued compliance.

7. Opt-out and suppression

You may opt out at any time by replying to any message with a removal request, or by emailing ahmed@radarledger.com. Opt-out requests are honoured same day: your details are added to a permanent suppression list which is checked before every outbound communication. Once suppressed, you will not be contacted again by any channel.

8. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Portability — request data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any right, email ahmed@radarledger.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. For Ireland: the Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

10. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or any form of visitor tracking. No data is collected from visitors to this website. No third-party requests are made when you load any page.

11. Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
