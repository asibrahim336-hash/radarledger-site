How it works

Watch

Daily automated crawls

Every morning, RadarLedger visits competitor storefronts and queries the Meta Ad Library. Raw HTML is archived; every data point has a timestamp and a source URL.

Score

Severity 0–10

Each detected change is scored. A 12% price drop on a hero product scores 9. A minor copy tweak scores 3. You decide your alert threshold; the default fires on 7 and above.

Deliver

Alerts and daily briefs

High-severity events trigger an immediate alert to your inbox. Everything else lands in a single daily brief — one page per competitor, sorted by severity.

What gets tracked

  • Price changes (up and down)
  • Promotional text and banners
  • Stock-outs and restocks
  • New product launches
  • Meta ad launches and stops
  • Offer and creative changes

Evidence discipline

Every claim RadarLedger makes is backed by a dated screenshot and a source URL. The raw HTML archive is replayable offline — if a detection cannot be reproduced from the archive, it does not ship.

We run the full detection pipeline against a control store every single day. The control store has a known mutation schedule: Monday a price drops 12%, Tuesday a promo changes, Wednesday a minor price rises, Thursday a product goes out of stock. If the system misses any of these, the build goes red and nothing is delivered to clients until it passes again.
