Competitor intelligence that arrives before your morning coffee.

RadarLedger monitors competitor pricing, promotions, and advertising activity for DTC e-commerce brands. When something changes, you get a scored alert. Every day, you get a snapshot.

How It Works

1

Detection

Automated daily crawls of competitor storefronts and the Meta Ad Library surface price changes, new products, stock-outs, promotion shifts, and advertising activity.

2

Scored Alerts

Every detected change is scored by severity. High-severity events (price drops ≥10%, stock-outs, new offers) trigger immediate alerts. Lower-severity changes are logged for your daily brief.

3

Daily Snapshots

A single-page summary per competitor: current pricing, recent activity, ad intelligence, and key findings — delivered to your inbox every morning.
